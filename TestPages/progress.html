TurnOver progress bar test

A progress bar that climbs from 0 to 100 percent, to hear how TurnOver reports one. Choose the setting first: NVDA+U in the NVDA keys, or Progress bars in the Verbosity list (VO-V) or in Settings.

Press a button, then move down to the bar so TurnOver reads it, as you would a download or a copy. With Beep, each percent beeps, rising in pitch. With Speak, the percentage is said every 10 percent, and "100 percent" at the end. Beep and speak does both.
