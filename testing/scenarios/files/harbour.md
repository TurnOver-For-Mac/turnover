# Harbour Guide

The harbour opens at **nine**. Boats wait outside the wall until the bell rings twice.

## Tides

- High water is about six hours after low water.
- Spring tides come after a full or new moon.

## Leaving

1. Call the harbour master on channel 12.
2. Keep the red buoy to port.

See [the tide tables](https://example.com/tides) for times.
